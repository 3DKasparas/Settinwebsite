About
Hi :), I am a setter from Lithuania currently living and working in Belgium. I've been routesetting for the past 5 years setting various international competitions and national team training camps. My main goal with routesetting is to explore movement and challenge the climber by putting them in uncomfortable positions. Besides setting I also absolutely love climbing. I am currently part of the Lithuanian national team and have competed in European and World cups on the World climbing circuit both for youth and adults. I also really enjoy climbing outside and get alot of inspiration from nature my current hardest accent is 8a/+
