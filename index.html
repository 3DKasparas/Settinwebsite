About
Hi :), I am a setter from Lithuania currently living and working in Belgium. I've been routesetting for the past 5 years setting various international competitions and national team training camps. My main goal with routesetting is to explore movement and challenge the climber by putting them in uncomfortable positions. Besides setting I also absolutely love climbing. I am currently part of the Lithuanian national team and have competed in European and World cups on the World climbing circuit both for youth and adults. I also really enjoy climbing outside and get alot of inspiration from nature my current hardest accent is 8a/+
Setting style
I really love to set all sorts of styles. Both old-school as well as modern dynamic moves interest me. Currently I am really motivated and push to set for a really high competition level so Iv'e been really enjoying setting huge coordinations and dynamic power boulders as well as sketchy and technical slow slabs. I feel pretty comfortable in climbing and testing as high as national competition level although i really believe that it takes a team to build and test boulders. I'm quite open minded and think that once a draft is built you should let go of ego and tweak the boulder as one big team. My current setting goal is to set for different international training camps and learn as much as possible in hopes of carrying this info and applying towards the lithuanian national team to push the level as high as possible.
